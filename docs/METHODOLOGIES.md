# Innovation methodologies

Reference list for the Programs section. Source: https://openinnovation.eu/04-02-2016/71-innovation-methodologies/ (page title says 71; the page itself lists **67**, and a reader comment on the page counted 67 as well). Names are written as the page prints them, including its spellings. The page gives only the name, focus stage and year; the author or source is shown in brackets in its list. The catalog labels (purpose) are our own proposal and can be changed.

Status: **16 of 67** are in the app. 5 more methodologies are in the app that are not on this list (see the end).

## Catalog: how methodologies are labelled

Every methodology has one or more **purposes** (what problem it helps with). The first purpose is the main one and gives the card its color; the others are shown as extra labels. Color is never the only signal: each label also has its name.

| Purpose | Label (EN) | Etichetta (IT) | Use it to... |
|---|---|---|---|
| strategy | Set direction and system | Definire strategia e sistema | decide where to innovate, build the innovation system, plan the portfolio |
| ideas | Generate ideas | Generare idee | find opportunities and come up with new ideas |
| customer | Understand customers | Capire i clienti | learn what people need and design offers around it |
| validate | Test and validate | Testare e validare | check a business idea quickly and cheaply before investing |
| develop | Turn ideas into projects | Trasformare idee in progetti | take approved ideas through to products, services or projects |
| open | Work with outside partners | Collaborare con l'esterno | involve partners, customers or the crowd |
| incentives | Engage and reward people | Coinvolgere e premiare | motivate people to take part (challenges, competitions, rewards) |

Each methodology also has a **stage** (from the source list): Seed (exploring), Early-stage, Growth, Maturity. It tells when in the life of an idea or company the method fits best.

Gap to fill: only two entries on the source list are mainly about incentives (Design Competitions, Innovation Challenge). Methods for idea reward and incentive systems will need to come from other sources.

## Adding a methodology

1. Add it to `TEMPLATES` in `app/src/lib/templates.ts` (purposes, stage, who it suits, activities) and any new items to `ITEMS`.
2. Add its name, description and activities (with a `guide`) to `tpl.<key>` in `en.json` and `it.json`.
3. Update the "In the app" column above.
